# National Freedom Studio research notes

## Initial findings from homepage inspection

The target site presents itself as **National Freedom Studio**, a recording studio brand with a strong editorial and underground music identity. The homepage metadata describes it as a landmark studio where some of the most original records of the last decade were made.

The visible information indicates a compact navigation structure with the sections **Studio**, **g3ar**, **disc0g**, **mast3ring**, **ab0ut**, and **cont4ct**. The stylized leetspeak naming is a recognizable part of the brand voice and should be preserved in the recreation.

The homepage appears to use a highly branded visual system with a buffalo-head emblem, multiple logo treatments, monochrome contrast, and a cinematic media-led hero area. A homepage video file is referenced at `https://nationalfreedom.studio/visuals/NF_home-vid_01_sm.mp4`, suggesting motion is central to the landing experience.

The structure inferred from the extracted content is a minimal landing page with a header, off-canvas or overlay menu, a hero/logo composition, one featured section titled `/// The Studio`, a call to action leading to the studio page, and a footer repeating the section links and studio credits.

## Brand cues to preserve

| Element | Observation |
|---|---|
| Naming style | Uses slashed headings and leetspeak labels such as `g3ar`, `disc0g`, `mast3ring`, `ab0ut`, and `cont4ct`. |
| Visual tone | Dark, stark, editorial, music-industry oriented. |
| Identity motifs | Buffalo mark, wordmark variations, monochrome/high-contrast palette. |
| Homepage behavior | Navigation plus media-led hero with concise copy and a section CTA. |
| Footer | Repeats navigation and credits the studio and site maker. |
